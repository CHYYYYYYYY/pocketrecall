      THE SERVICE / NO APP TO MANAGE

      Less searching.
      More being there.

      PocketRecall is designed to live behind your glasses: notice everyday objects, remember their last-seen places, and answer when you ask. No dedicated phone app to open, inventory to maintain, or dashboard to check.

      This website is our glasses simulator and evidence viewer. It exposes what the service observes, remembers and says, so you can inspect the experience that would usually stay in the background.

      WHO WE ARE DESIGNING FOR

      A little help, in familiar places.

      01 / EVERYDAY LIFE

      The everyday forgetter.

      Keys, glasses, a wallet. Find the things you put down while your attention was somewhere else.

      02 / EVERYDAY INDEPENDENCE

      Older adults.

      A simple spoken way to recall where familiar belongings were last seen, for people who find keeping track of them harder.

      03 / EVERYDAY WORK

      Warehouse teams.

      Recall where tools, supplies and shared items appeared across a busy shift, with time and image evidence to check.

      LOOKING AHEAD / PHYSICAL INTELLIGENCE

      A shared memory of the physical world.

      The same object, place and time records could help a future robot or embodied agent answer “Where was it?” before deciding what to do. Our next direction is connecting human observations to robot perception, spatial reasoning and assistance through a shared memory interface.

      Product direction, not a deployed robot capability. Reliable tracking, cross-device map alignment and physical-world evaluation are next steps.
